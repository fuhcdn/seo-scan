# Refund Policy (Digital Products)

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026

---

## 1. Overview

This Service is a one-time digital product (the AI SEO Audit Report). Due to the nature of digital products, once delivered they cannot be "returned"; therefore refunds are handled on the basis of fairness and good faith.

## 2. Circumstances Eligible for a Refund

Users may apply for a full or partial refund in the following circumstances:

1. **Non-delivery:** If, within 48 hours of payment, the Company fails to deliver the report (not due to incomplete user information), a full refund is available.
2. **Material delivery defect:** If the report cannot be opened due to a system error, its content is materially incomplete, or it does not match the service ordered, then after verification by the Company, the user is entitled to either a full refund or a free re-run (one of the two).
3. **Order cancellation:** After payment and before delivery, the user may request cancellation and obtain a full refund.

## 3. Circumstances Not Eligible for a Refund

1. The report has been successfully delivered with no material defect;
2. The user's expectation of a specific ranking result is not met;
3. The audit cannot be completed because the user-provided audit target is missing, incorrect, or inaccessible (in such cases a re-run may be negotiated).

## 4. Application Procedure

1. Email your application to refund@seoscanaudit.com within **7 days** after delivery;
2. Provide the order number, proof of payment, and the reason for the refund;
3. The Company will make its best effort to respond within **5 working days** of receiving the application.

## 5. Refund Method

Refunds are returned to the original payment method; actual crediting time depends on the payment service provider (generally 5–10 working days).

## 6. Final Decision

The Company reserves the final decision on refund applications, provided that all decisions are fair, reasonable, and consistent with consumer protection principles.
