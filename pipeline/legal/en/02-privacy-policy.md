# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026
**Governing law:** The Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486 of the Laws of Hong Kong, "PDPO")

---

## 1. Data We Collect

Given the nature of this Service, users must submit a website URL for the audit. We may collect the following data:

1. **User account data:** name, email address, and payment information.
2. **Audit target data:** the URL submitted by the user and the public content it contains. If a web page contains personal data (as "personal data" defined under the PDPO), such data is subject to this Policy.
3. **Usage data:** browser type, IP address, and access times, for security and analytics purposes.

## 2. Purposes of Data Use

1. To provide, operate, and improve the audit Service;
2. To process payments and deliver reports;
3. For legal compliance, security, and fraud prevention;
4. To send service updates after consent (opt-out available at any time).

## 3. How Data Is Collected

We collect only the data necessary to complete the audit and follow the principle of data minimisation. We do not actively collect or solicit special categories of personal data (such as health, race, or political opinions).

## 4. Data Retention

After the audit is completed, unless otherwise required by law or agreed to by the user, we will delete or de-identify the collected audit target data within a reasonable period.

## 5. Disclosure of Data

We do not sell personal data. We disclose it only in the following circumstances:

1. To processors necessary for the Service (e.g., hosting, payment, and email providers), bound by contractual confidentiality obligations;
2. To comply with legal obligations or government requests as required by law;
3. To protect the legitimate rights and interests of the Company or third parties.

## 6. Data Transfers

If data is transferred out of Hong Kong, we will ensure the transfer complies with PDPO safeguards, including the use of processors providing an equivalent level of protection.

## 7. Your Rights (PDPO)

Under the PDPO, you have the right to:

1. **Access** your personal data;
2. **Correct** inaccurate personal data;
3. Make inquiries or complaints regarding our processing.

Please email privacy@[your-domain].com to exercise these rights. We will respond within 40 days of receiving the request.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted transmission, access controls, and employee confidentiality obligations.

## 9. Third-Party Websites

The reports or this website may contain links to third-party websites, whose privacy policies are independent and unrelated to the Company.

## 10. Minors

This Service is not intended for persons under 18 years of age, and we do not intend to collect personal data from minors.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

If there are material changes, we will publish the update on the website and notify you where necessary.
