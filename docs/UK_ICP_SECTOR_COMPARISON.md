## TOP THREE ICP RECOMMENDATIONS

### #1 — Commercial Fit-Out / Refurbishment Contractors

**Profile:** 20–100 employees, London + major UK cities, shop fitting / office fit-out / dilapidation works

**Why they may buy:**
- Every project is worth £50K–£5M — a single new enquiry from the website pays for the audit many times over (HYP)
- Their website is their portfolio — if it doesn't show competence, tenders are lost before they bid (HYP)
- Decision-maker = managing director or commercial director, no procurement gate for £400 (HYP)
- The website IS the tender pre-qualification — a weak site means they don't get shortlisted (HYP)

**Why they may not buy:**
- Many get work through relationships and framework agreements, not the website (HYP)
- Busy project delivery cycle — owner may not have time to read a written report (HYP)
- May already have an agency or in-house marketing person (UNKNOWN)

**Biggest risk:** Response rate may be very low because they are project-busy and website feels secondary to relationships (HYP)

**Template angle:** **A — Website Decision-Priority** (their website IS the tender pre-qualification)

---

### #2 — Wholesale / Industrial Distributors

**Profile:** 10–100 employees, regional or national, industrial supplies / PPE / HVAC equipment / electrical wholesale

**Why they may buy:**
- Moving from catalogue/phone ordering to online quote is a live strategic issue for this sector (HYP)
- Their websites are often product-catalogue-heavy but enquiry-path-poor — perfect diagnostic target (HYP)
- Lower competitive noise from SEO agencies than professional services (HYP)
- B2B email culture — unsolicited written approaches are normal in wholesale (HYP)

**Why they may not buy:**
- Product catalogue websites are complex to diagnose — may need more pages than our standard scope (HYP)
- Many still rely on field sales reps and existing accounts — website feels secondary (HYP)
- Some are subsidiaries of larger groups — decision may not sit locally (UNKNOWN)

**Biggest risk:** Website complexity may exceed our delivery scope for the price point (HYP)

**Template angle:** **B — Evidence-Led Diagnostic** (their problem is too much catalogue, not enough decision path)

---

### #3 — Commercial Office Cleaning

**Profile:** 10–100 employees, London + major cities, contract office cleaning

**Why they may buy:**
- Contract cleaning tenders are won or lost on trust — the website is the trust builder before the tender (HYP)
- Very high volume of SMEs in this sector — large addressable market (FACT: SIC 74.70)
- Owner-operated — fast decision speed (HYP)
- Low competitive noise — fewer SEO agencies target this sector vs accountants/lawyers (HYP)

**Why they may not buy:**
- Lower average contract values than fit-out — may feel £400 is expensive for a "website check" (HYP)
- Lower digital sophistication — may not see the value of a written diagnostic (HYP)
- Many rely on辗转介绍 and local reputation, not website (HYP)

**Biggest risk:** May not see enough value in a written document to pay US$397 (HYP)

**Template angle:** **C — Written No-Call / No-Pressure** (their market is trust-sensitive and they value directness)
