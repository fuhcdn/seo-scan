# OUTREACH PLAYBOOK — Text-only（等 owner 批內容+名單先發）

> 零電話/零 face-camera/零買名單/零 fake claim。每條 message 有 opt-out。分批。

## 渠道優先序
1. **Email**（有真決策者 email;最高接受度)
2. **LinkedIn DM**（無 email;owner 賬號）
3. **Contact form**（無 email 無 LinkedIn;文案短）
4. ~~WhatsApp~~（除非對方公開 WhatsApp Business 並屬合適文化——低優先)

## Message 原則
- 首行講「我睇咗你網站,觀察到 X」——**用真 observation,唔係 generic**
- 一個 observation + 一句點解影響 enquiry + 一句我哋做法
- CTA: 「要唔要我哋免費幫你做一個 5 分鐘嘅 conversion 斷位診斷(無需付款)」——**免費 mini-insight 做 lead magnet,唔硬銷 US$497**
- 簽尾:真名+seoscanaudit.com+opt-out
- 長度:email <120 字;LinkedIn <80 字

## Email Template V1（等批）
```
Subject: [Company] — one thing on your website that may be costing enquiries

Hi [First name],

I was reviewing [industry] websites in [region] and noticed something on
[their domain]: [1-sentence specific observation, e.g. "your quote form
sits behind two clicks and there's no visible response-time promise"].

For a quote-based business like yours, that step is usually where
enquiries quietly drop.

We build evidence-led SEO decision reports (every recommendation is tied
to one of your own pages, with owner/QA/verification steps). If it's
useful, I can send you a free 5-point conversion diagnosis for
[their domain] — no payment, no obligation. If you like what you see,
the full report is US$497.

Want me to send the free diagnosis?

[Name]
seoscanaudit.com
(If this isn't relevant, just reply "no" and I won't email again.)
```

## LinkedIn DM V1（等批）

```
Hi [First name] — I reviewed [domain] and noticed [observation].
I run a small service that turns that kind of finding into a

Happy to send a free 5-point diagnosis first — no obligation. Interested?
```

## Contact form V1（等批）
```
Quick one: I noticed [observation] on [domain]. We prepare evidence-led
SEO decision reports (US$497, 24h). Happy to send a free 5-point
conversion diagnosis first — just reply to this or email hello@seoscanaudit.com.
```

## Follow-up（Day 2,1 次）
```
Just floating this back up — happy to send the free diagnosis whenever
useful. If now's not the time, no problem at all.
```

## 目標量（三日）
- 批准後 Day 1:20-30(email 15-20 + LinkedIn 8-12)
- Day 2:follow-up 未回者
- Day 3:改良 variant 20 個
- 總計 ~60-80 觸達;benchmark 期望 1-5% reply → 2-6 回覆;合理機會 ≥1 qualified

## Anti-spam/合法
- 不買名單;只手工搜集公開資料
- 每訊息 personalise 至少 1 項 observation
- opt-out 明確;「no」即停
- 不寄任何虛假訊號;不假冒公司;不裝熟
